How a row is priced

Implied value = TVL × 0.22 × growth multiplier × age multiplier × late multiplier × chain multiplier. Growth is the seven-day change clipped to −50%…150%; age is days since the record began, capped at 1095 days; a record older than 1460 days loses up to 25% because a long run with no token is weaker news. No protocol is special-cased.
